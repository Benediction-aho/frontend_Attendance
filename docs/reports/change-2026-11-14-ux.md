## Details

### UXC-01 – High – Retroactive weekly report for admins on day one
- Role and device: admin, iPhone. Screenshot `j1-03-today-iphone.png`.
- Steps: go live on a non-working day (`admin_attendance_starts_on` = 15 Nov, a Sunday), then sign in as an admin before any check-in.
- Expected: "Weekly report: Not required". The admin was not tracking during the week of 9–13 Nov (rule 5: no retroactive effect).
- Observed: "Weekly report – To send – Before 16 Nov 2026, 5:00 PM", with the "Send my weekly report" button.
- Cause: in `WeeklyWindow::deadlineFor`, a person who was not expected on Friday gets the "absent Friday" grace until their next expected day. In `DashboardService::weeklyReportStatus`, `open` is matched before `expected` is checked. A person who did not track attendance on that Friday (an admin before the start date, and probably an employee created after it) is therefore shown "To send".
- Real-world impact: with a Monday go-live (Q31), every admin sees "To send" on their first day. `targetWeek` probably also accepts the upload. This was not verified on the UI because the scratch DB settings changed afterwards.
- Fix (backend): skip the grace when `isExpected` is false because the person was not tracking or not a member that day. Return `not_expected`.

### UXC-02 – Medium – 24-hour time in the check-in card
- Admin and employee, all devices. Screenshot `j1-03-today-android.png`. The card says "after 08:10" right below a clock that reads "12:56:35 PM". This is UI-1 from `change-2026-11-14-ui.md`, still present in the build.

### UXC-03 – Medium – New tab hidden on small phones
- Employee, Android 320. Screenshots `j2-01-work-tabs-android320.png` and `j2-06-stale-delete-android320.png`.
- The "Activity reports" tab starts at x = 325 on a 320 px viewport, so it is fully off-screen with no fade. A hurried user does not find the feature. The bottom bar shows "Notificatio…". The admin bar at 320 (Today, My work, Notifications, Admin, Sign out) fits.

### UXC-04 – Low – Correcting a wrong PDF at the limit
- Employee, Android 320. After 5 reports, deleting one keeps "You have sent today's 5 activity reports". The confirm dialog says so, which is correct and clear, but there is no way to send the corrected file today. Decide under Q37.

### UXC-05 – Low – "employees" in the super admin daily count
- Super admin, desktop. The text "0 of 4 employees recorded work" covers 2 employees + 2 admins. Use "people".

### UXC-06 – Low – Lowercase brand term
- Admin, Android. "Waiting for you – Nothing to review. See face id devices". This comes from `DashboardPage.tsx:195` (`first.label.toLowerCase()`).

### UXC-07 – Low – Non-working day figures
- Admin, iPhone (Sunday still non-working). Screenshot `j1-06-checked-in-iphone.png`. After checking in: "Days present 1 of 0". Prefer "1 (extra day)", or hide "of 0".

### UXC-08 – Low – Upload button at the limit
- Employee. Screenshot `j2-04-limit-android320.png`. The quota message is clear. Make the button outline or disabled so the user does not open a form they cannot send.

## Verified OK
- **Admin first sign-in** (code → password → profile skip → Face ID setup) works and lands on Today. The nav shows Today / My work / History / Notifications + Administration. On phones the bar is Today / My work / Notifications / Admin / Sign out.
- **Admin check-in** with GPS + Face ID: late status, dashboard refreshed. My work is locked before check-in with a clear message and unlocked after.
- **Activity reports, check-in gate:** before check-in the form shows "Check in first" and Send is disabled. A forced send gets 403 and goes back to Today with "Please check in first to use the app today."
- **Activity reports, validation:** empty submit gives two field errors. A title with a link or an e-mail address, a text file renamed .pdf, a .docx (also warned before sending) and a PDF with JavaScript are each refused with a clear English message. A 150-character title with emoji and accents and a 180-character file name are accepted and wrap correctly on 320 px.
- **Daily limit:** "1 of 5 sent today · 4 left", then "You have sent today's 5 activity reports…".
- **Delete:** the confirm dialog gives the time ("until 1:29 PM"). Deleting after the window from a stale page gets a clear toast, and the button is gone after reload.
- **Admin reading reports:** an admin with "View reports" receives employees' activity-report notifications. Opening another admin's report, a deleted report or an unknown id shows "This report is not available" (404). Opening an employee's report works.
- **Super admin:** dashboard and attendance totals include admins ("Admin" badge), the activity list includes admins' reports with the badge, and notifications for admins' uploads arrive. Settings > Attendance shows "Admins check in from" as a date with a clear explanation and "Default: empty".
- **Profile photo:** uploaded through the crop sheet, it shows right away and after reload. `files.show` answers `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin`, and other API responses answer `same-site`. Note: :4214 and :8034 are same-site, so the real cross-site case is covered by the header check only.
- **PDF links** (`Open PDF`) download with 200 `application/pdf`.
